Privacy Policy

Lino's Services LLC, Kalamazoo, Michigan. Effective November 24, 2026.

Who we are

Lino's Services LLC is a locally owned company in Kalamazoo, Michigan, serving Kalamazoo, Portage, Battle Creek, Grand Rapids and all Southwest Michigan. You can reach us at 269-599-1111 or cars4cashservices@gmail.com.

What information we collect

Only what you choose to send us when you ask for a quote or hire us:

  • Your name and phone number.
  • Details about the service you need, for example the car you want to sell or the cleanout you need done, and the address where the work will happen.
  • Photos you send us of a vehicle or a space, when you send them to get an accurate quote.

You send this information when you call, text, message us on WhatsApp or Facebook, email us, or use the quote form on this website. The form does not store anything on this website: it opens your own email app, and you send the message yourself.

How we use your information

  • To answer you and prepare your free quote.
  • To schedule, carry out and follow up on your service.
  • To keep basic records of the jobs we have done.

What we never do

We never sell your information. We do not share it with third parties for marketing, and we do not share it with anyone else except when the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not collect information about your visit.

Your choices

At any time you can call or email us and ask what information we have from you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We will take care of it right away.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with its new effective date.
